Nightbloom Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Nightbloom is a self-help mental wellness app. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your data.

Journal entries and check-ins

Your journal entries and mood check-ins are stored only on your device, using local storage. They are never sent to us or to any third party. We have no server that receives this data, no accounts, and no way to see what you write.

Companion chat

Companion is an AI chat feature. When you send a message, it's transmitted to our server and forwarded to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a reply. We do not store the content of your messages beyond what's needed to process that single request. Our server only retains a daily message count per IP address, used to prevent abuse; it does not retain message text. Anthropic's own privacy policy governs how they process the request on their end.

What we don't do

  • We don't require or collect an account, email, or name.
  • We don't use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.

Links you choose to open

The Resources section includes crisis line phone numbers and external websites. Tapping these opens your phone's dialer, messaging app, or browser, which are then governed by that app's own privacy practices, not ours.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact information listed on our app store listing.
