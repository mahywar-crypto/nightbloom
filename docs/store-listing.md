## Full description

Nightbloom is a gentle companion for anxiety, panic, overthinking, and the
days everything just feels like a lot. It's not a replacement for therapy,
but it's there whenever you need a moment to steady yourself.

**What's inside:**

🌙 **Mood check-ins** — A quick, guided way to notice how you're feeling and
what might be behind it. Get gentle, relevant suggestions afterward based on
your mood.

📓 **Private journal** — Write freely or use a rotating prompt. Everything
stays on your device. Nothing is ever uploaded.

🌬️ **Breathing exercises** — Guided box breathing with a calming visual, for
moments you need to slow down.

🆘 **Panic attack SOS** — A step-by-step grounding sequence (the 5-4-3-2-1
technique plus guided breathing) for when panic hits and you need something
to hold onto.

💬 **Companion** — Feeling lonely or just want to talk? Chat with an AI
companion anytime. It's not a therapist and says so upfront, but it's there
when you need to talk something through.

🎵 **Calming sounds** — Ambient sound to help you relax or focus.

📚 **Learn** — Clear, credible information about common mental health
conditions like anxiety, depression, OCD, and more, written to help you
understand, not to diagnose.

🤝 **Crisis resources** — Real hotline numbers and support directories,
front and center, for whenever more help is needed.

Nightbloom keeps your journal and check-ins fully private and local to your
device. The only feature that talks to a server is Companion chat, and that's
disclosed clearly in the app.

*Nightbloom is a self-help tool, not a substitute for professional diagnosis
or treatment. If you're in crisis, please contact your local emergency
number or a crisis line right away.*
